Rafting Company

As a company our purpose is to deliver unforgettable whitewater adventures that builds confidence, forge connection, and reconnect people with the raw power of nature safely,every time. Our mission is to be the most trusted rafting outfitter, known as much for our safety records as for the thrill we deliver. Our motto is "Ride the Rush".

History

Whitewater Rafting Company started in 2010 as two friends and a secondhand raft chasing weekend thrills on the river. What began as casual trips quickly grew as more people wanted in, and by 2013 it became official built on one promise, real whitewater adventure, done safely. Since then, we've grown into a full team of certified guides with professional-grade gear, but the mission hasn't changed: get people out of their routines and onto the water for an experience worth remembering.
